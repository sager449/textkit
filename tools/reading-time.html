What is a reading time calculator?

A reading time calculator is a free online tool that estimates how long it will take to read a piece of text. It uses the average reading speed of an adult (about 200 words per minute) to give you a realistic time estimate — instantly, as you type.

Writers, bloggers, editors, and content creators use reading time calculators to show readers how long an article will take. Adding "5 min read" to a blog post improves user experience, reduces bounce rates, and helps readers decide whether to read now or save it for later.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste your text into the box above.
  2. The reading time appears automatically — no button needed.
  3. See the word count and character count alongside the reading time.
  4. Click Copy to copy the text, or Clear to start over.

When to use a reading time calculator

Reading time estimates are useful in many situations:

  • Blog posts — show "5 min read" at the top of each article.
  • Medium and Substack — both platforms display reading time automatically.
  • Newsletters — help subscribers decide when to read.
  • Academic papers — estimate how long reviewers will need.
  • Speeches and scripts — plan timing for presentations.
  • Podcast scripts — estimate spoken duration.
  • Audiobooks — get a rough idea of narration length.

Average reading speeds

  • Slow reader: ~150 words per minute
  • Average adult: ~200–250 words per minute
  • Fast reader: ~300–400 words per minute

FAQ

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is calculated by dividing the total word count by an average reading speed of 200 words per minute.

Is this reading time calculator free?

Yes — 100% free, with no signup or registration required.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server.

Does it work with Arabic text?

Yes. The calculator works with both English and Arabic text, including right-to-left scripts.

What's the ideal reading time for a blog post?

Most readers prefer articles between 3 and 7 minutes. Posts under 3 minutes feel too short, while anything over 10 minutes may lose casual readers.

Does reading time include images and videos?

No. The calculator only measures text. Images and videos usually add extra time depending on how long readers spend on them.
