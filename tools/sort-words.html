What is a sort words tool?

A sort words tool is a free online utility that takes every word in your text and rearranges them alphabetically. Choose A→Z for ascending or Z→A for descending. Words are separated by spaces, and sorting is case-insensitive and locale-aware — so Arabic words sort correctly too.

Sorting words is useful in language learning, vocabulary building, keyword research, and any task that requires alphabetical order. Manually sorting hundreds of words is slow and error-prone. This tool does it in one click.

How to use it

  1. Paste or type your text into the box above.
  2. Click Sort A → Z or Sort Z → A.
  3. The words are sorted instantly and joined with single spaces.
  4. Click Copy to copy the result, or Clear to start over.

FAQ

What does this tool do exactly?

It takes every word in your text and sorts them alphabetically — A to Z or Z to A — separated by spaces.

Is this sort words tool free?

Yes — 100% free, with no signup or registration required.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server.

Does it work with Arabic text?

Yes. The tool uses locale-aware sorting, so Arabic words are sorted correctly according to Arabic alphabetical order.

Does it remove duplicate words?

No. Duplicates are kept and sorted normally. If you want to remove duplicates first, use the Remove Duplicate Words tool.
