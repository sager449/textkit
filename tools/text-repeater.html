Text Repeater

Repeat your text multiple times with a custom separator.

What is a text repeater?

A text repeater is a free online tool that duplicates your text a specific number of times. You choose how many times to repeat it, and what separator to place between each copy — a space, comma, dash, newline, or anything else.

Text repetition is useful for testing layout and design, filling forms with placeholder content, generating long strings for stress tests, creating decorative patterns, and playing with text on social media. This tool does it instantly, up to 1000 repetitions.

How to use it

  1. Enter your text in the big box.
  2. Set the Times field to how many copies you want (1 to 1000).
  3. Set the Separator — leave it as a space, or use ,, -, |, or a newline.
  4. Click the Repeat Text button.
  5. Click Copy to copy the result, or Clear to start over.

FAQ

What does this tool do exactly?

It repeats your text a specified number of times, joined by a custom separator (space, comma, newline, or anything you choose).

Is this text repeater free?

Yes — 100% free, with no signup or registration required.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server.

What's the maximum repeat count?

You can repeat text up to 1000 times. The result size depends on your original text length.

Can I use a newline as separator?

Yes. Paste a line break into the separator field, or type \n — the tool will interpret it as a newline.
