What is a remove numbers tool?

A remove numbers tool is a free online utility that deletes all numeric digits from your text. It removes English digits (0-9) and Arabic-Indic digits (٠-٩) in one click, while keeping letters, spaces, and punctuation intact.

Numbers are useful in many contexts, but they cause problems in others. Text analysis, word frequency counting, natural language processing, and password generation all benefit from number-free text. This tool does that instantly.

How to use it

  1. Paste or type your text into the box above.
  2. Click the Remove Numbers button.
  3. All digits are removed instantly — letters and punctuation stay.
  4. Click Copy to copy the clean text, or Clear to start over.

FAQ

What does this tool do exactly?

It removes all numeric digits from your text — both English digits (0-9) and Arabic-Indic digits (٠-٩). Letters, spaces, and punctuation are kept.

Is this remove numbers tool free?

Yes — 100% free, with no signup or registration required.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server.

Does it remove Arabic numbers?

Yes. It removes both Western Arabic numerals (0-9) and Eastern Arabic-Indic numerals (٠-٩).

Does it remove numbers inside words?

Yes. Any digit anywhere in the text is removed, even if it's part of a word like 'mp3' or 'iPhone12'.
