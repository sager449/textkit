What are Arabic numbers?

Arabic-Indic numerals (٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩) are the digit symbols traditionally used in Arabic-speaking countries across the Middle East and North Africa. They're completely different symbols from the "Arabic numerals" (0123456789) that are used worldwide — despite the confusing name, Western digits actually originated from India via Arab mathematicians.

Both sets of numerals coexist in the modern Arabic-speaking world. Some countries use Western digits (0-9), others use Arabic-Indic (٠-٩), and many use a mix. Converting between the two is essential when working with cross-platform data, search engines, or software that only recognizes one format.
