About TextKit

TextKit is a free collection of 34 text tools that work directly in your browser — no signup, no upload, no waiting.

Our Mission

We believe simple tools should stay simple. Every tool on TextKit is designed to solve one specific problem — cleanly, fast, and without distractions.

What You Can Do

  • Clean and format text
  • Count words, characters, and sentences
  • Convert between formats (Base64, URL, JSON, HTML)
  • Extract emails, URLs, numbers, and hashtags
  • Work with Arabic and English text

Privacy First

Your text never leaves your browser. All processing happens locally on your device — we don't store, see, or share your content.

Free Forever

Every tool is 100% free. No hidden fees, no subscription required.
