What is a Case Converter?

A case converter is a free online tool that changes the capitalization of your text. Whether you need everything in UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, or Sentence case — you can do it in one click.

It's useful for writers, developers, students, and anyone who needs to fix the capitalization of copied text quickly.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste your text into the box above.
  2. Choose the case you want: UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, or Sentence case.
  3. Click Copy to copy the result.

The four case types

  • UPPERCASE — EVERY LETTER IS CAPITALIZED.
  • lowercase — every letter is small.
  • Title Case — Every Word Starts With A Capital Letter.
  • Sentence case — Only the first letter of each sentence is capitalized.

  • Private — everything runs in your browser

FAQ

Is this tool free?

Yes — 100% free, with no signup required.

Does it work with Arabic text?

Yes. Arabic doesn't have case, but the tool will not break your text. It works perfectly with English and other Latin-based languages.

Is my text safe?

Yes. All processing happens locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.

Can I convert a large document?

Yes. There's no character limit — you can paste entire documents.
