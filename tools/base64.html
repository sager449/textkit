What is Base64?

Base64 is a method of encoding binary data (like images, files, or text) into a text format made of 64 safe characters — A-Z, a-z, 0-9, +, and /. It's widely used in web development, email attachments, and APIs.

Encoding your text in Base64 is useful when you need to safely transmit data through systems that only support plain text.

How to use this tool

  1. Type or paste your text into the box above.
  2. Click Encode → Base64 to convert to Base64.
  3. Click Decode ← Base64 to turn Base64 back into readable text.
  4. Use Copy to copy the result.

FAQ

Is Base64 encryption?

No. Base64 is encoding, not encryption. Anyone can decode Base64 back to the original text. Never use it to protect sensitive data.

Does it work with Arabic text?

Yes. Our tool uses UTF-8 encoding, so Arabic, Emoji, and other special characters work perfectly.

What is Base64 used for?

Common uses: encoding images in CSS/HTML, email attachments (MIME), API authentication, and storing binary data in JSON.

Is my text safe?

Yes. All encoding and decoding happens locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.
