What is a remove spaces tool?

A remove spaces tool is a free online utility that cleans up extra whitespace from your text. It removes double spaces, replaces tabs with single spaces, and trims leading and trailing whitespace — all with a single click.

Copied text often contains hidden problems: extra spaces between words, tabs from code editors, invisible whitespace at the beginning or end. These small issues cause big problems in programming, databases, and formatted documents. A remove spaces tool fixes them instantly.

How to use it

  1. Paste or type your text into the box above.
  2. Click the Remove Spaces button.
  3. Extra spaces, tabs, and leading/trailing whitespace are removed instantly.
  4. Click Copy to copy the cleaned text, or Clear to start over.

FAQ

What does this tool do exactly?

It removes extra spaces from your text: double spaces become single, tabs are replaced, and leading/trailing whitespace is trimmed.

Is this remove spaces tool free?

Yes — 100% free, with no signup or registration required.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server.

Does it remove all spaces or just extra ones?

By default it removes extra spaces and keeps single spaces between words. This preserves readability while cleaning up messy text.

Does it work with Arabic text?

Yes. The tool works with both English and Arabic text, including right-to-left scripts.
