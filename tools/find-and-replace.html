Find and Replace

Replace any text with something else — instantly.

What is a find and replace tool?

A find and replace tool is a free online utility that searches your text for a specific word or phrase and replaces every occurrence with something else — instantly, in one click.

Unlike the find-and-replace built into most editors, this tool is browser-based and requires no installation. It's perfect for quick edits, cleaning data, renaming variables in code, fixing typos, and translating between British and American spellings.

How to use it

  1. Type the text you want to find in the first input.
  2. Type the text you want to replace it with in the second input.
  3. Paste your text into the big box.
  4. Click the Replace All button. Every occurrence is replaced instantly.
  5. Click Copy to copy the result, or Clear to start over.

Why use our find and replace tool?

  • Free — no signup, no limit
  • Private — your text never leaves your browser

When to use it

  • Text editing — rename a character or place in a story.
  • Data cleaning — replace inconsistent values in a dataset.
  • Code refactoring — rename variables across a file.
  • Translation — swap between British and American spellings.
  • Formatting — convert dashes, quotes, or spacing consistently.
  • Form letters — replace placeholders with real values.

FAQ

What does this tool do exactly?

It finds every occurrence of a word or phrase in your text and replaces it with something else — in one click.

Is this find and replace tool free?

Yes — 100% free, with no signup or registration required.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server.

Is it case-sensitive?

Yes. The tool matches case exactly — 'Hello' and 'hello' are treated as different words.

Does it support regular expressions?

Not currently. The tool does plain text replacement. Future versions may add regex support.
