What is URL encoding?

URL encoding (also called percent encoding) is the process of converting characters that aren't allowed in URLs into a safe, transportable format. For example, a space becomes %20, the character & becomes %26, and Arabic characters like ع become multi-byte sequences like %D8%B9.

URL decoding is the reverse — it converts percent-encoded strings back into readable text. This is essential when reading URLs, debugging web requests, or cleaning scraped data.

Common examples

  • hello world → hello%20world
  • a&b=c → a%26b%3Dc
  • مرحبا → %D9%85%D8%B1%D8%AD%D8%A8%D8%A7
  • user@email.com → user%40email.com

FAQ

What is URL encoding?

URL encoding (percent encoding) converts characters that aren't allowed in URLs into a safe format. For example, a space becomes %20, and ع becomes %D8%B9.

Is this URL encoder free?

Yes — 100% free, with no signup or registration required.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server.

What's the difference between encodeURI and encodeURIComponent?

This tool uses encodeURIComponent, which encodes all reserved characters — perfect for encoding query parameters and form values.

Does it work with Arabic text?

Yes. Arabic characters are correctly encoded to their UTF-8 percent-encoded form, and decoded back to Arabic.
