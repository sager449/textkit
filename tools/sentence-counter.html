What is a sentence counter?

A sentence counter is a free online tool that counts the number of sentences in your text. It detects sentence-ending punctuation marks — like periods, question marks, and exclamation points — and gives you an instant count as you type.

Writers, students, editors, and translators use sentence counters to check readability, meet assignment requirements, and improve the rhythm of their writing. Short sentences are easier to read; long sentences can confuse. Knowing your average sentence length helps you write clearer text.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste your text into the box above.
  2. The sentence count appears automatically — no button needed.
  3. See also the word count, character count, and average words per sentence.
  4. Click Copy to copy the text, or Clear to start over.

When to use a sentence counter

Sentence counting is useful in many everyday situations:

  • Academic writing — essays and papers often require a specific number of sentences per paragraph.
  • Readability checks — shorter sentences (15–20 words) are easier to read.
  • Editing and proofreading — spot overly long sentences that need splitting.
  • Translation — compare sentence counts between source and target text.
  • SEO writing — short sentences improve mobile readability and time on page.
  • Teaching — help students understand sentence structure.

FAQ

How does the sentence counter work?

The counter splits your text at sentence-ending punctuation marks (. ! ? ؟) and counts the resulting sentences.

Is this sentence counter free?

Yes — 100% free, with no signup or registration required.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server.

Does it support Arabic punctuation?

Yes. The counter recognizes both English (. ! ?) and Arabic (؟) sentence-ending punctuation.

What is a good average words per sentence?

Most readability experts recommend 15–20 words per sentence for clear, easy-to-read writing.

Does it count sentences without punctuation?

No. A sentence without ending punctuation is treated as part of the previous sentence. Add a period, question mark, or exclamation point to separate sentences.
