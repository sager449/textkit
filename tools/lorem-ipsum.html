What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is placeholder text used by designers, developers, and printers since the 1500s. It comes from a Latin text by Cicero, but the words have been scrambled so they don't form a coherent message — this is intentional. The goal is to fill a layout with realistic-looking text without distracting the viewer from the design itself.

Today, Lorem Ipsum is used in almost every design tool and mockup — Figma, Sketch, Adobe XD, WordPress themes, and website templates — because it looks like natural language but carries no meaning.
