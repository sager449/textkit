Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

At TextKit, your privacy is important to us. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Your Text Never Leaves Your Device

All text processing happens locally in your browser. When you use any of our tools (like Remove Spaces, Word Counter, or Base64), the text you enter is processed on your device. It is never sent to our servers, never stored, and never seen by anyone — including us.

2. What We Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your text content
  • Personal information (name, email, address)
  • Files you process

We do collect anonymous usage data via Google Analytics:

  • Page views and general usage patterns
  • Browser type and device type
  • Country (approximate location)
  • Referral source (how you found us)

This data is anonymized and helps us improve the site.

3. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for:

  • Theme preference (light/dark mode saved locally)
  • Google Analytics (anonymous statistics)
  • Google AdSense (for displaying ads)

4. Advertising

We may display ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at: Google Ads Settings.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics — for anonymous statistics
  • Google AdSense — for displaying ads
  • Vercel — for hosting the website

6. Children's Privacy

TextKit is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at: contact@textkit.com
