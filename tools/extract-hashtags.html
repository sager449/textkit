What is a hashtag extractor?

A hashtag extractor is a free online tool that scans any text — a social media post, a caption, an article, a spreadsheet — and pulls out every hashtag it contains. The result is a clean, deduplicated list, one hashtag per line.

Whether you're analyzing trending topics, auditing your own social media strategy, comparing competitors, or building a hashtag library, extracting hashtags manually is slow. This tool does it in one click.

How to use it

  1. Paste your text with hashtags into the box above.
  2. Click the Extract Hashtags button.
  3. All unique hashtags appear instantly, one per line.
  4. Click Copy to copy the list, or Clear to start over.

FAQ

What does this tool do exactly?

It scans your text, finds every hashtag (starting with #), and returns a deduplicated list — one hashtag per line.

Is this hashtag extractor free?

Yes — 100% free, with no signup or registration required.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server.

Does it work with Arabic hashtags?

Yes. The extractor recognizes both English hashtags like #tech and Arabic hashtags like #تقنية.

Does it remove duplicate hashtags?

Yes. Each unique hashtag appears only once in the output. The stats panel shows both the total count and the unique count.
