What are Latin diacritics?

Latin diacritics are small marks added above, below, or through letters to change their pronunciation. They appear in many languages: French (é, è, ê, ç), Spanish (á, é, í, ó, ú, ñ), German (ä, ö, ü), Portuguese (ã, õ, ç), Italian (à, è, ì, ò, ù), and many others.

These accents are essential for correct pronunciation — but they cause problems in URLs, filenames, usernames, databases, and code. That's where this tool comes in: it removes accents while keeping the base letter, so café becomes cafe and naïve becomes naive.

How to use it

  1. Paste or type your accented text into the box above.
  2. Click the Remove Diacritics button.
  3. All accents are removed instantly — base letters stay intact.
  4. Click Copy to copy the clean text, or Clear to start over.

FAQ

What does this tool do exactly?

It removes accents and diacritics from Latin letters. For example, café becomes cafe, naïve becomes naive, and résumé becomes resume.

Is this remove accents tool free?

Yes — 100% free, with no signup or registration required.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server.

Does it work with German, Spanish, and French?

Yes. It removes accents from all Latin-script languages including French (é), Spanish (ñ), German (ü), and Portuguese (ã).

Will it change the letter ß (German sharp S)?

No. The ß character has no accent mark, so it stays as-is. If you need to convert ß to ss, use a different tool.
